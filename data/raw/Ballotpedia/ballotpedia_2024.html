Endorsements from party leaders are one tool candidates can use to distinguish themselves. According to political strategist Susan Del Percio, “Endorsements do matter; what you do with them is more important. They need to be treated as a way of targeting the group you seek to persuade, especially on certain issues.”[1] Endorsements can also help inform voters about their ballot. Northeastern University Political Science Department Chairman Costas Panagopoulos said, "Generally speaking, voters look for information shortcuts in order to make political decisions at times and endorsements can provide some of those cues."[2]

This page provides an overview of noteworthy endorsements in the 2024 presidential election. For the general election, this page provides cross-party endorsements from elected officials and all endorsements issued by members of Congress, governors, attorneys general, and secretaries of state. This page also provides 2024 Republican presidential primary endorsements from current and former presidents and vice presidents, current and former party leaders, governors and other state executives, members of Congress, mayors of large cities, and state legislative majority and minority leaders. Primary endorsements were compiled by FiveThirtyEight.

An endorser may have multiple entries in the tables below if they changed the candidate they endorsed.
